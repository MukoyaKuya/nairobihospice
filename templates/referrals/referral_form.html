Triage Priority Protocol

Emergency (< 24 Hours)

Severe intractable pain crisis, acute spinal cord compression, acute airway compromise, or massive bleeding.

Urgent (< 48 Hours)

Rapid functional decline, severe persistent nausea/vomiting, malignant bowel sub-obstruction, acute caregiver distress.

Routine (< 5 Days)

Stable advanced illness, long-term palliative trajectory, ongoing counseling, and home care coordination.
